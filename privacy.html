Privacy Policy

Last updated: DATE

This policy explains how SwotSpot ZA ("we", "us") handles information when you visit our website.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or give us personal details to use the site. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and we use them only to reply to you.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show adverts. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising.

Analytics

We may use tools such as Google Search Console or Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they use. This data is collected in aggregated form.

Third-party content

Our pages embed videos from YouTube. YouTube may set its own cookies when you play a video. Please see Google's privacy policy for details.

Children

Our content is made for learners, but the website is intended to be used with the guidance of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We handle any personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). To ask about or remove information you have sent us, email YOUR_EMAIL.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
