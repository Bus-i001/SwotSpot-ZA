About SwotSpot ZA

SwotSpot ZA is a free study website for South African learners in Grade 8 and 9. We make clear explanations, mind maps and worksheets with memos so learners can revise at their own pace, on any phone or computer.

Who runs SwotSpot ZA

SwotSpot ZA is run by an independent creator in South Africa. I started it because good revision material should be easy to find, easy to understand and free to use, especially for learners who study on a phone.

What we cover

We start with Grade 8 and 9 and plan to add more grades over time.

  • CAPS: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Natural Sciences, Technology, English, Afrikaans, History and Geography.
  • IEB: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Afrikaans, History and Geography.

How our material is made

Every topic is written in simple language and follows the topics in the CAPS and IEB curricula as closely as we can. Each topic page has a short written explanation, a video lesson on our YouTube channel, a mind map and a worksheet with a memo. If you find a mistake, tell us and we will fix it.

How to use a topic page

  1. Watch the video and read the explanation.
  2. Study the mind map and print it or save it.
  3. Try the worksheet without looking at the memo.
  4. Check your answers with the memo and revise anything you got wrong.

Why there are adverts

Everything on SwotSpot ZA is free. Advertising helps cover the cost of running the site so we can keep adding new topics without charging learners or parents.

Please note

SwotSpot ZA is an independent website. It is not affiliated with the Department of Basic Education or the IEB. Our material is a study aid and does not replace your school textbook, teacher or official exam guidelines.
