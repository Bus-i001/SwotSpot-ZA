Study timetable generator

Make a weekly study timetable in under a minute. Fill in the boxes, tap Make my timetable, then print it or save it as a PDF.

A session of 30 to 45 minutes with a short break works well for most learners.

Advertisement

How to use your timetable

  • Print it or save it as a PDF, then stick it where you study.
  • Tick each session off when it is done.
  • In each session, use active methods such as testing yourself. See How to study effectively.
  • Review the timetable once a week and adjust it to match your real life.

Preparing for an exam? Read How to prepare for an exam and How to create an exam study timetable.

Everything you type stays on your device. We do not store or send your timetable.
